# Lifestack Skills Reference

Complete documentation for all Lifestack skills.

## Overview

Lifestack skills form a pipeline for identity-first personal development:

```
/vision → /map → (daily action) → /checkpoint → /pivot → /commit → /retro
                                                    ↑
                                    /journal  /mentor  /unblock
```

## Pipeline Skills

### /vision (Phase 1 — Active)

**Purpose:** Declare your identity and craft a vision statement.

**Persona:** Identity Architect — warm, precise, never prescriptive.

**When to use:** At the start of your Lifestack journey, or when your identity
has evolved significantly enough to warrant a new declaration.

**Duration:** 20-45 minutes (this is deep work, not a quick form).

**Output:** `~/.lifestack/vision.md` — your identity declaration with actions,
mindset patterns, success criteria, constraints, first wedge, anti-goals,
origin story, and target date.

**Conversation flow:**
1. Identity Excavation — discover who you are at your best
2. Core Desire — the deep why beneath the identity
3. Identity Actions — 5 specific recurring actions
4. Identity Mindset — thought patterns for difficulty, choice, and failure
5. Success Criteria — observable evidence of identity alignment
6. Resources & Constraints — what helps and what hinders
7. First Wedge — one action you can take in the next 24 hours
8. Anti-Goals — what this identity explicitly rejects
9. Origin Story — why now
10. Target Date — when to evaluate (8-12 weeks recommended)

---

### /map (Phase 2)

**Purpose:** Break your vision into a goal tree with weekly actions.

**Persona:** Strategic Life Planner.

**When to use:** After `/vision`, to translate identity into action plans.

**Output:** Goal files in `~/.lifestack/goals/`

---

### /checkpoint (Phase 2)

**Purpose:** Measure your Identity Alignment Score.

**Persona:** Alignment Analyst — compassionate but unflinching.

**When to use:** Daily or weekly, to measure how well actions match identity.

**Output:** `~/.lifestack/checkpoints/{date}.md`

**Scoring:**
- Action Completion (40%): Did you do what you said?
- Identity Congruence (30%): Do actions reflect your identity?
- Directional Momentum (20%): Moving closer to vision?
- Self-Awareness (10%): Honest self-assessment quality

---

### /commit (Phase 2)

**Purpose:** Lock in commitments for the next cycle.

**Persona:** Commitment Architect.

**When to use:** After `/checkpoint` or `/retro`, to set next-cycle actions.

**Output:** `~/.lifestack/commitments/current.md`

**Format:** Implementation intentions — "When [trigger], I will [action]"

---

### /retro (Phase 2)

**Purpose:** End-of-cycle retrospective and learning.

**Persona:** Reflective Guide.

**When to use:** End of each cycle (weekly recommended).

**Output:** `~/.lifestack/retros/{date}.md`

---

### /pivot (Phase 2)

**Purpose:** Re-evaluate goals while preserving identity.

**Persona:** Strategic Advisor for goal evolution.

**When to use:** When a goal isn't working or feels misaligned.

**Output:** Updated goal files in `~/.lifestack/goals/`

**Outcomes:** Persist (adjust), Replace (new goal), or Retire (identity evolved)

---

## Support Skills

### /journal (Phase 2)

**Purpose:** Daily identity-anchored journaling.

**Persona:** Journaling Companion.

**When to use:** Any time, for daily reflection.

**Duration:** 5-10 minutes.

**Output:** `~/.lifestack/journal/{date}.md`

---

### /mentor (Phase 2)

**Purpose:** Coaching from your future self.

**Persona:** The user's Future Self.

**When to use:** When seeking guidance, motivation, or perspective.

**Output:** Conversation only (no file output).

---

### /unblock (Phase 2)

**Purpose:** Diagnose and resolve psychological resistance.

**Persona:** Resistance Specialist.

**When to use:** When you know what to do but can't make yourself do it.

**Resistance types:** Fear, perfectionism, overwhelm, values conflict, skill gap,
environmental friction, identity incongruence.

**Output:** Conversation with one immediate micro-action.

---

## Common Patterns Across All Skills

All Lifestack skills share these interaction patterns:

1. **Identity Anchoring** — Read `~/.lifestack/vision.md` first, reference identity throughout
2. **Future Self Questioning** — "The version of you who has already achieved this..."
3. **Evidence Collection** — Ask for concrete actions, not feelings
4. **Compassionate Confrontation** — Honest, never shaming, always forward-looking
